Product guidance

Is Touring Performance right for you?

Best for

Fitness paddles, longer coastal or flat-water routes and experienced riders who value tracking, glide and pace over maximum width.

Consider another option if

Choose another board for SUP yoga, equipment-heavy fishing or if you want the widest and most forgiving platform for a first paddle.

Why this design

Its 14-foot length, 30-inch width, V hull and speed tail favour directional efficiency and sustained glide.
