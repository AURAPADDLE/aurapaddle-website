Product guidance

Is Gannet right for you?

Best for

Surfers progressing from beginner fundamentals towards confident intermediate and advanced surfing who want responsive shortboard handling, a broad size ladder and the flexibility of a five-fin setup.

Consider another option if

Choose another board if you need the soft construction and generous stability of a first-lesson board, or the trim and glide of a longboard.

Why this design

Eight catalogue sizes support a more considered fit, while the five-fin layout allows different fin configurations as conditions and preferences change.
