Wide inflatable paddle boards

Choose usable width, not width alone.

Yoga Cruiser is 36 inches wide — 91.44 cm, or about 91 cm — with an 11-foot outline and a broad deck intended for yoga, gentle movement and relaxed cruising. CoastGo is 33 inches wide and 10'6" long, giving it a more compact all-round format.

SUPs for family outings

Plan who paddles, and whether you take turns.

For one board shared in turns, compare the size and load of each paddler, the water you will use, and how the package gets from the car to the launch. CoastGo includes a carry bag and hand pump. Yoga Cruiser includes a wheeled bag and an electric pump that needs a compatible car power socket.

  • Taking turns on one board: choose for every intended paddler, not only the lightest or most experienced person.
  • Paddling at the same time: plan a suitable board and safety equipment for each paddler.
  • Carrying a passenger: do not infer approval from spare capacity. Maximum capacity is a load limit, not a recommended rider weight or a tandem-use rating.

Add each paddler's clothing and gear when checking load. If your use involves a passenger, ask AURA for model-specific guidance before ordering.

Before ordering

Common comparison questions.

Does maximum capacity equal recommended rider weight?

No. It is a load limit. Count the rider, clothing and everything carried, then consider board shape, experience and conditions.

Does the pre-order total equal the amount due today?

No. The card separates the full product total from the 50% initial payment due today.

Is shipping included in these totals?

Do not assume it is. The cart or balance request confirms shipping under the current rules for the selected destination.

Do all packages use the same pump and bag?

No. CoastGo lists a hand pump and carry bag. The other three list an electric pump and wheeled bag.
