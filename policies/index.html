Customer care

Returns & Refund Policy

Australian Consumer Law: Our products come with consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded. You may be entitled to a repair, replacement or refund for a major failure, and compensation for other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage.

30-day change-of-mind returns

In addition to your statutory rights, you may request a change-of-mind return within 30 calendar days after delivery. To qualify, the product must be unused on water, clean, undamaged, complete with all accessories, and returned in its original or equivalently protective packaging in resalable condition.

Contact us before sending anything back. If approved, we will provide return instructions. Change-of-mind return freight is paid by the customer, and original delivery charges are not refundable unless required by law. We recommend using tracked and insured transport because the customer is responsible for the item until it is received by us.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect products

If a product is faulty, damaged, not as described or incorrect, contact us with proof of purchase and evidence of the issue. We will assess the claim and provide a remedy required by the Australian Consumer Law. Where a product has a major failure, you may choose a refund or replacement. For a non-major problem, we may choose to repair the product within a reasonable time; if we cannot, you may be entitled to a replacement or refund.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. We normally initiate the refund within five business days after approval, but your bank or payment provider may take additional time to display it.

Two-year product warranty

Our advertised two-year warranty applies to manufacturing defects under normal recreational use and is additional to, and does not replace, Australian Consumer Law rights. It does not cover ordinary wear, cosmetic damage, punctures, misuse, impact damage, improper inflation, unauthorised repair, commercial or rental use, poor storage, prolonged sun exposure, or failure to follow care and safety instructions.

Conditional production

Pre-order Policy

Current pre-order offer: Each eligible pre-ordered board receives an AUD $50 incentive deducted from its confirmed standard retail price. Customers pay 50% of the applicable pre-order price when ordering and the remaining 50% before dispatch. Yoga Cruiser Glacier Blue and all three CoastGo colours are confirmed pre-orders with no minimum production quantity. Other participating products remain conditional on their published target.

Production campaign targets

  • Confirmed pre-orders — no minimum: Yoga Cruiser Glacier Blue and all three CoastGo colours proceed without a production threshold.
  • Paddle Board Launch Batch 01 — 50 boards combined: Yoga Cruiser Matte Sandstone, Eucalyptus Green and Coral White, Angler Fishing and Touring Performance contribute to one shared target, regardless of model or colour.
  • Wake & Foil — 5 units per product: Vela Wakeboard, Inflatable Hydrofoil and Hydrofoil Set each enter production when that individual product reaches 5 paid units.
  • Surf & Commercial — 10 boards per product: Gannet, Current, Meridian and Wayfinder each have their own 10-board target. Every size and colour within the same product contributes to that product total.

Information shown before payment

Before a live pre-order opens, its product page states the selected SKU, standard price, pre-order price, 50% initial payment, whether production is confirmed or conditional, and the estimated dispatch date. Conditional campaigns also display their target quantity, initial-payment board count and closing date. Campaign counters include successfully paid, non-refunded initial-payment board quantities only.

Payment, incentive and confirmation

A 50% initial payment is charged when the live pre-order is placed. The AUD $50 incentive applies to each eligible pre-ordered board before the payment is divided. A successful initial payment reserves the selected product; for a conditional board pre-order it also contributes to the published target. Before dispatch, AURA PADDLE sends a separate secure payment request showing the remaining 50%, confirmed shipping charge and payment due date. Payment details are not automatically reused for the second payment. Dispatch occurs only after the requested balance has cleared. Products without a confirmed retail price remain waitlist-only and cannot accept payment.

Change-of-mind cancellation before dispatch

A customer may cancel a pre-order within 48 hours of the successful initial payment and receive a full refund of the amount paid. The cancellation request must be emailed to admin@aurapaddle.com; the time we receive the email determines whether the request is within the cancellation period.

For a conditional pre-order, a customer may also cancel for a full refund after the first 48 hours until AURA PADDLE confirms production in writing. If the applicable target is reached early, production may be confirmed early and this cancellation window will end when that written confirmation is issued. Once production has been confirmed or the order has been placed with the manufacturer, change-of-mind cancellation is not available.

Confirmed pre-orders with no minimum quantity are already allocated to confirmed production and therefore have the 48-hour cancellation period only. These voluntary change-of-mind terms do not limit any rights or remedies available under the Australian Consumer Law.

If the target is not reached

If the applicable campaign target is not reached by 30 September 2026, that campaign will be cancelled. AURA PADDLE will initiate a full refund of the initial payment to the original payment method, normally within five business days. No balance or shipping charge will be requested for the cancelled order. The customer's bank or payment provider may take additional time to display the refund.

Material changes or delays

If a confirmed production campaign experiences a material specification change or delay, affected customers will be notified and given the available options, including a refund where required by the Australian Consumer Law or the terms of the campaign.

Necessary storage does not build an email list. Optional analytics can measure aggregate visits and navigation, while optional marketing technologies can support campaign measurement or relevant advertising.

No optional analytics or advertising tracker is currently active on this offline website version. If these technologies are added, they must remain off until the visitor chooses the relevant category in Cookie Settings. A visitor can reject optional technologies, change their choice later, or clear saved website data through their browser. .

Access, correction, retention and complaints

You may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including tax, accounting, dispute and warranty records, then delete or de-identify it where practical.

For a privacy request or complaint, email admin@aurapaddle.com. We aim to acknowledge privacy complaints within five business days and respond within a reasonable period. If applicable and you are not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
