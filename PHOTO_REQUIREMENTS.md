# AURA website redesign — photography requirements

The local redesign preview uses only images already present in the website repository. The following photographs are still required before production launch.

## Priority 1 — homepage and range navigation

1. **Master brand hero**
   - Wide landscape, 16:9 and 21:9 crops.
   - One authentic AURA scene showing more than one way of using the water: paddle, surf, wake or foil.
   - Leave clean negative space on the left for the headline.
   - Golden-hour or early-morning light; premium, calm and natural rather than extreme-sports advertising.

2. **Surf range category image**
   - Rider on an AURA Gannet, Current or Meridian.
   - Landscape 4:3 and portrait 4:5 crops.
   - Board branding must remain readable.

3. **Commercial category image**
   - Surf instructor and student using an AURA Wayfinder.
   - Show safe instruction and an authentic surf-school environment.
   - Landscape 4:3 and portrait 4:5 crops.

## Priority 3 — supporting brand content

- Australian team/founder portraits.
- Product design and testing behind-the-scenes images.
- Dealer, school and rental partner photographs.
- Care-guide sequence: inflation, rinsing, drying, rolling and storage.
- Genuine customer lifestyle images with model releases.

## Delivery specification

- Supply original high-resolution files plus web-ready exports.
- Preferred master format: high-quality JPEG or TIFF; PNG for isolated products with transparency.
- Minimum long edge: 3000 px for product images, 4000 px for hero images.
- Include horizontal 16:9, standard 4:3 and vertical 4:5 crops where requested.
- Avoid embedded text; all website copy will remain live HTML.
- Record photographer credit, usage rights, product/model name, colourway and location in the file metadata or delivery sheet.
