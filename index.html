Our Story

We built the board
we couldn't find

We searched every surf shop from Bondi to Byron. The choice was always the same: cheap boards that flex and fade under the Australian sun, or hard boards that don't fit in the car, the apartment, or the life we actually live.

Aura Paddle was created to close that gap. A board worthy of Australian waters — in terms of performance, UV protection, and the way it looks leaning against your balcony at 7am.

We started with the yoga community. We noticed that women — the most serious SUP yoga practitioners — were consistently under-served by boards designed for a different paddler. Aura Yoga Cruiser was built around them, and built beautifully.

What We Stand For

The principles behind
every Aura board

01

Designed for Australian conditions

Every specification — from UV coating to EVA thickness — was chosen with Australian coastlines in mind. Not adapted from a European catalogue. Designed here, for here.

02

Stability without compromise

A 36″ platform is a statement: we believe you shouldn't have to sacrifice stability for a board that performs, packs away, or looks beautiful. You can have all three.

03

Honest materials, honest pricing

Woven drop stitch. Dual-layer PVC. Stainless D-rings. We don't spec down to hit a price point. AUD $799 is the right price for what's in the box — not inflated, not deflated.

04

The portability promise

Every Aura board must fit in a standard car boot when packed. No excuses. This constraint shapes every design decision — and it's why our users actually use their boards.
