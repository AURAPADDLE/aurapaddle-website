The AURA direct pre-order model

Less in between.More board for your money.

Australian brand. Factory direct. Built to confirmed demand.

AURA PADDLE works directly with our manufacturing partners and produces selected boards against confirmed pre-orders. By removing distributor mark-ups and reducing excess inventory, we lower the cost of getting each board to the water—and pass that value on through better pre-order pricing.

01 / DIRECT

Direct, not distributed

From our production partners to AURA PADDLE riders, without distributor layers.

02 / DEMAND

Made to confirmed demand

Paid pre-orders guide production, reducing unsold stock and warehouse overhead.

03 / VALUE

Savings shared

You commit early. We reward that commitment with better pre-order pricing.

Pay 50% of the pre-order price when ordering, then the remaining 50% and confirmed shipping charge through a separate secure payment request before dispatch. Production targets and estimated dispatch dates are shown on each participating product page. Transit time begins after dispatch. If a conditional target is not reached, the initial payment is fully refunded to the original payment method.
