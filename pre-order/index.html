Your protection

Every product page states whether its pre-order is confirmed or conditional.

Confirmed pre-orders have no minimum quantity. If a conditional batch does not reach its stated target by the closing date, the affected order is cancelled and fully refunded to the original payment method.

The complete process

Five clear steps from choice to dispatch.

Every participating product page shows its price, production condition and estimated dispatch date before you place an order. Conditional batches also show their target, live count and closing date.

01 / CHOOSE

Select a participating model, size and colour.

Products marked “Pre-order” belong to a clearly identified production campaign.

02 / PAY 50%

Pay half of the pre-order price at checkout.

For eligible pre-order boards, the AUD $50 incentive is applied first, then the reduced pre-order price is divided into two equal payments.

03 / RECORD

Your initial payment reserves the board.

A successfully paid conditional order joins its published batch count; a confirmed pre-order requires no minimum quantity.

04 / CONFIRM

We confirm production and your final amount.

Before dispatch, we send a separate secure request for the remaining 50% and confirmed shipping charge.

05 / PAY & DELIVER

Complete the balance before dispatch.

Your board is dispatched after the remaining balance clears. Delivery timing remains an estimate and is updated as production and freight progress.

Common questions

What customers usually want to know.

Product-specific specifications, colours, prices and current batch progress remain on the individual product page.

Can different paddleboard colours count together?

Yes. Yoga Cruiser Matte Sandstone, Eucalyptus Green and Coral White, plus Angler Fishing and Touring Performance, contribute to the shared 50-board target. Glacier Blue is a separate confirmed pre-order.

Is the AUD $50 incentive already included?

Yes, where shown for eligible pre-order boards. The product page displays the reduced pre-order price and the standard price before checkout.

When do I pay?

You pay 50% of the pre-order price when ordering. Before dispatch, AURA sends a separate secure request for the remaining 50% and confirmed shipping charge. The original payment method is not charged automatically.

Are the dispatch dates guaranteed?

No. Once production is confirmed in writing, estimated dispatch is approximately six weeks later. The dates shown on individual product pages are current estimates. If a conditional target is reached early, production and dispatch may be brought forward; timing may also change if production or freight conditions change. We will keep affected customers updated, and transit time begins after dispatch.

What if I change my mind and cancel my pre-order?

You may cancel within 48 hours of the successful initial payment for a full refund. For a conditional pre-order, you may also cancel for a full refund after that period until AURA PADDLE confirms production in writing. Once production has been confirmed or the order has been placed with the manufacturer, change-of-mind cancellation is not available. Confirmed pre-orders with no minimum quantity have the 48-hour cancellation period only. This policy does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Which pre-orders have no minimum?

Yoga Cruiser in Glacier Blue and all three CoastGo colours are confirmed pre-orders. They receive the AUD $50 incentive but do not depend on a production target.
