The complete process

Five clear steps from choice to dispatch.

Every participating product page shows its price, amount due today and current estimated dispatch basis before you place an order.

01 / CHOOSE

Select a participating model, size and colour.

Products marked “Pre-order” identify the exact SKU and terms that will be recorded with the order.

02 / PAY 50%

Pay half of the pre-order price at checkout.

For eligible pre-order boards, the AUD $50 incentive is applied first, then the reduced pre-order price is divided into two equal payments.

03 / START

Your successful initial payment starts the allocation.

There is no shared minimum quantity and no public production counter for new pre-orders.

04 / CONFIRM

We confirm production and your final amount.

05 / PAY & DELIVER

Complete the balance before dispatch.

Your board is dispatched after the remaining balance clears. Delivery timing remains an estimate and is updated as production and freight progress.

Common questions

What customers usually want to know.

Product-specific specifications, colours, prices and current payment terms remain on the individual product page.

Do different orders have to reach a target together?

No. A new pre-order starts its own production allocation after the initial payment succeeds.

Is the AUD $50 incentive already included?

Yes, where shown for eligible pre-order boards. The product page displays the reduced pre-order price and the standard price before checkout.

When do I pay?

You pay 50% of the pre-order price when ordering. Before dispatch, AURA sends a separate secure request for the remaining 50% and confirmed shipping charge. The original payment method is not charged automatically.

Are the dispatch dates guaranteed?

No. New pre-orders show an estimated dispatch of approximately 10 weeks after successful initial payment. We will keep affected customers updated about material changes, and transit time begins after dispatch.

What if I change my mind and cancel my pre-order?

You may cancel within 48 hours of the successful initial payment for a full refund. Once production has been placed or confirmed for that order, change-of-mind cancellation is not available. This does not limit Australian Consumer Law rights. Existing paid orders retain the cancellation protection recorded when they were placed.

What happens to an older target-based order?

It stays under its recorded terms. Its price, target protection, dates, payments and refund rights are not replaced by the new public offer.
